Privacy

The contact form is used to respond to photography inquiries.

What we process

The name, email, additional contact details, city, shoot type, preferred date and message you choose to submit.

Why we process it

Only to discuss your inquiry and arrange a shoot. The website does not subscribe you to mailing lists or use advertising trackers.

Service providers

The form is processed by Cloudflare Workers and protected by Cloudflare Turnstile. Your inquiry and contact details are delivered to the photographer through Telegram. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Storage and deletion

Inquiries are delivered to the photographer’s private Telegram chat. You may request access, correction or deletion using the contact below. Do not submit sensitive information through the form.

Local preferences

The website stores your language preference in a functional cookie. Form protection may use technical browser data. No additional analytics is connected.
